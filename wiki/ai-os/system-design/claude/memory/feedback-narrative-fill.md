# Narrative-fill: challenge the story before it steers

**The pattern:** when facts are missing (a silence, a delay, an unexplained decision), Julian constructs a narrative about other people's motives or politics and then treats it as a read of the situation - "Tony as detractor", "it's game over", "the family-radar lever is working". Confirmed as the most broadly evidenced of his confirmed biases in the 19 Sep 2026 history scan: enacted across TTI politics, the inheritance conflict, and job post-mortems. Full card: [[mm-narrative-fill-bias]] (wiki mirror of pattern + evidence).

**Why memory and not just the skill:** this bias acts in ordinary conversation, not only at decision points - it can fire in any discussion that touches another person's behaviour, which makes it easy for the bias-check skill's situational triggers to miss. It earned the always-loaded slot on frequency and stakes: the narratives drive real strategy toward real people.

**How to apply:**
- When Julian presents a read of someone's motives or state of mind, ask what is observed versus constructed. If load-bearing details are unchecked, say so before building on the read.
- Offer the boring explanation (busy, on holiday, process running on its own clock) alongside the dramatic one.
- When a speculative narrative is still useful, label it SPECULATION explicitly in any record so it cannot harden into fact.
- Watch for narrative escalation under anxiety: "no reply yet" becoming "it's over" is the pattern firing, not information arriving.
