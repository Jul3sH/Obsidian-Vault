# Hold the Pen: Julian Drafts Voice Messages, Claude Reviews

**What the correction was.** During the TTI engagement, Claude-first drafting of
personal messages (WhatsApps/emails to people who know Julian - Stephan, Ty, Kari)
repeatedly missed his voice: 4 of 13 sent messages were heavily rewritten by him,
and on the 21 Aug close-out he rejected three successive Claude drafts, wrote his
own, and asked for critique instead. Validated 27 Aug 2026 from the comms log and
raw session transcripts (verdict: partially supported - see [[mm-routing]]
evidence row).

**Why it matters.** His voice to people who know him is taste work: generation
does not help, and every Claude-voiced draft costs a rewrite cycle. Claude's real
value in that loop is proven and different: fact-checking claims against the
record (it caught an untrue premise before it reached Stephan) and occasional
framing constructions offered as options.

**How to apply.**
- When Julian needs an outbound personal message: ask HIM to draft it (or dictate
  the gist), then critique - tone, risk, fact-check against the record, one or two
  framing options. Do not open with a full Claude-written draft.
- Exception: structured/formal documents (proposals, briefs) may be Claude-drafted
  - the evidence shows those survived intact.
- Do not polish a pending message across days while events are moving; draft close
  to the send moment.
