# No hyphen connectors in outbound prose

**The correction.** While drafting the Ty engagement proposal (10 Aug 2026), Julian had to ask four separate times for spaced hyphens to be removed and replaced with commas or other punctuation. AGENTS.md bans em and en dashes and lists " - " as a permitted substitute, but Julian's actual standard for polished outbound documents is stricter: no hyphen connectors at all.

**Why it matters.** Hyphen-connected clauses read as an AI-writing tell and as unfinished drafting in a document a Deputy CFO will scrutinise. Repeated cleanup passes also cost Julian time and trust: each one he catches himself undermines confidence in the rest of the text.

**How to apply.** At drafting time, in any reader-facing prose (proposals, letters, messages, documents Julian will send onward): connect clauses with a comma, colon, full stop, or parentheses instead of " - ". Compound words (cross-region, 12-month) keep their hyphens. Internal working notes and wiki plumbing may still use " - " per AGENTS.md, but if Julian flags them in a file, sweep that file completely and verify with a search rather than asserting. Related: [[feedback-plain-writing-not-riddles]].
