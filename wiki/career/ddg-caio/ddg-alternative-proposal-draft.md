# The Faster Route to an AI-Native DDG

**A proposal from ACME Company Limited**

## Where you are

DDG is profitable, data-rich and entrepreneurial. Your edge is proprietary sports data and the people who know how to trade on it. Around that edge sits an operational layer (reporting, admin, process, seven development teams) that you suspect costs more than it returns, and an AI opportunity you know is real but haven't yet landed.

Your current plan is to pay 3 to 5 consultants for 6 to 10 weeks each, watch them audition, and maybe appoint a Chief AI Officer at the end. The instinct behind it is exactly right: assess people through real execution, not interviews. The format is the problem.

## What the audition format actually buys you

Run the numbers on your own plan: 30 to 50 consultant-weeks of spend, five people pulling in five directions, each given controlled access, each needing onboarding, security vetting and management attention from leaders who would rather be running the business. At the end you hold opinions and prototypes built to win a competition, not to run your company. Then the real work starts.

Meanwhile the thing you actually want (fewer bottlenecks, leaner operations, AI applied to pricing and data, decisions made faster) has not moved.

## The alternative: skip the audition, start the work

Engage one accountable company on outcomes. Not a hire, not a headhunt, not a beauty parade. You tell us the biggest constraint in the business right now. If you can't name it in a sentence, that is itself the finding, and stage one surfaces it fast. Then we remove it, prove the approach on real DDG problems, and repeat. The company changes organically, one removed constraint at a time, which is how entrepreneurial businesses actually change.

We see two places the value lives, and we'd expect to work both:

1. **The operational layer.** Most of what it does can be redesigned around AI agents and a smaller number of capable generalists: reporting, management cadence, routine process. Roles are removed or upgraded one at a time as systems prove themselves, not in a big-bang restructure.
2. **The core engine.** Applying current AI capability to what actually makes DDG money: sports data products, pricing and odds research, and the decision loops around them.

## How it runs

| Stage | Length | You pay | You get |
|-------|--------|---------|---------|
| 1. Find and prove | 2-3 weeks | [HK$ fixed fee] | The bottleneck named and quantified, a working demonstration against it, and a plain-language plan. Kill it here and keep everything. |
| 2. Remove the constraint | 4-6 weeks | [HK$ fixed fee, part contingent on the agreed outcome] | The constraint measurably gone, at least one production-quality AI workflow live, your team using it. |
| 3. Keep compounding | Monthly, fractional | [HK$ monthly retainer] | Ongoing senior AI leadership without a C-suite salary: next constraint, then the next. Cancel any month. |

Each stage gates the next. You can stop at any point and keep the assets, the systems and the findings. If the work stops after stage one, you still walk away with something the audition process was never going to give you: a costed, evidenced view of what to build or who to hire, ready to take to market or to tender if you choose.

## Who you'd be dealing with

- **[Dean], Hong Kong.** [One-line credential: pragmatist, builds and ships AI systems for real businesses.] On the ground, in your office, the person you hold accountable.
- **[Julian], UK.** [One-line credential: 25+ years architecting enterprise technology, now applying AI to organisational design.] Architecture, decision systems and the operating model, working UK hours that overlap your HK afternoon and evening.

One company, one throat to choke, senior people only. No juniors learning on your time.

## The honest question

"Where have you done exactly this before?" Nobody has: genuinely AI-native operating companies are being invented right now, and anyone claiming a long track record of building them is selling you something. What we bring is decades of making enterprise technology and organisations actually work, current hands-on AI systems practice, and a commercial structure where we only fully win when your constraint is gone. Your IP stays yours, access is controlled and auditable from day one, and everything we build is documented so you are never dependent on us.

## Next step

Thirty minutes with us both. Bring the constraint that annoys you most; we'll bring what we'd do about it in the first fortnight.
