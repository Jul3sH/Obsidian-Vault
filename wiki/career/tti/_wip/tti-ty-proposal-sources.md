# TTI — Ty Proposal: Source List

## What this is, and why it exists

**What it is:** every factual claim in [[tti-ty-engagement-proposal]] that rests on external evidence, with its primary source, the verbatim wording, and whether it is safe to say to TTI. One place to check a claim rather than searching six files.

**Why it was created (9 Aug 2026):** Julian asked where the evidence for two opening claims was logged. Answering took four searches across three files, and surfaced two defects nobody had spotted - a requirement citing a summary instead of the primary transcript, and a claim used in the proposal that had never been logged at all. A single source list makes both classes of error visible.

**How it is used:** Julian only. Check a claim before saying it; check the whole list before sending. `send: NEVER`.

> **⚠ As of 9 Aug 2026: this is now a set of THREE paired files.** [[tti-ty-engagement-proposal]] (what Ty reads, authoritative on content) · [[tti-ty-call-script]] (what Julian says, authoritative on delivery) · this file (what backs it up). **An edit to any claim requires checking all three in the same session.**

**Sourcing tags** (same scheme as [[tti-ea-requirements]]): **PUBLIC** = externally published, safe to reference without explaining how Julian knows it · **DIRECT** = said to Julian personally · **INTERNAL** = employee town hall, *never quotable* · **OWN** = Julian's own record.
