# TTI Ty Engagement Proposal - Codex Review - 2026-08-07

## 1. Ty's 60-second reaction

I understand the shape: you want to work on initiatives I already own, not sell me a side programme. Good. The hooks are mine: global P&L, IT cost leverage, structural G&A, AI in workflows. The fee is not insane for a US$15.3bn company, and the NPV math is fine.

But I am still being asked to commit US$320k for 12 months to an outsider with no TTI track record, no break clause, and no measured benefit yet. Month 1 says it will set the targets, which means I am committing before the numbers exist. That is the problem.

The document also tells me what "enterprise architecture" is around, but not enough what you will do on Monday morning. If I have to explain this to Tony or Tom, I need fewer concepts and one live operating sequence.

## 2. Findings

### FATAL

| Finding | What is wrong | Why it matters to Ty | Specific fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1. The no-break 12-month commitment is not de-risked.** | The document says month 1 sets targets, but the buyer is already committed to the remaining 11 months before those targets exist. That reads like "pay me to discover whether this pays." | A CFO can defend a 12-month bet if the bought output is clear. He cannot easily defend a no-break commitment to an unproven outsider where the value case is measured after signature. Tony only has to say: "So we are locked in before he knows the benefit?" | Recast month 1 as execution, not discovery. Name the first 30 days as three concrete deliverables plus one live decision put through the mechanism. Example: "By day 30 you have the cross-region decision map, the first FinOps cost view, the governance charter draft, and one selected live decision moved to decision owner, ratifier and date." The 12-month commitment must buy an operating build, not an audit that decides whether the build is worth doing. |

## 3. Locked-decision Risk

| Locked decision | Exposure | Mitigation within the lock |
|---|---|---|
| **No break clause.** | This is the core fatal risk. Without an exit, month 1 cannot be framed as "we will see if it is worth doing." | Make the commitment buy specific execution outputs regardless of what the baseline finds. Month 1 should set targets, but also deliver working governance on one live issue. |
| **No 6-month option.** | Ty may feel choice has been removed after Stephan asked for 6 or 12. | The "why 12 not 6" paragraph is good. Tighten it to structural proof: two quarterly cycles are needed to test whether governance holds without Julian pushing every decision personally. |
| **Do not anchor above HK$2.5M.** | The fee is defensible but leaves no concession room. | Keep the basis exactly as written. It is finance-reviewable. Do not add market multiples. |
| **Organising message stays: work on existing initiatives in an EA context.** | It can sound like positioning rather than work. | Translate it into one operational sentence: "I take one cross-region decision already stuck in your system and make it resolve through named owner, standard, ratifier and date." |

## 4. The Single Change That Most Improves the Odds of a Yes

Add a short "first 30 days" section that makes the 12-month no-break commitment feel like execution, not discovery.

It should answer:

- Which live decision is the proof case?
- Which data does Julian request in week 1?
- Which forum or committee does he map first?
- What does Ty receive by day 10 and day 30?
- What decision right, ratifier and escalation rule are drafted by day 30?

This is the sentence Ty needs to remember: "I am not buying a theory of enterprise architecture. I am buying a mechanism that takes one stuck cross-region technology decision and gives it an owner, a standard, a ratifier and a date."

## 5. Verdict

**Send with the FATAL fix. Do not send as-is.**

The proposal is close. The hooks are accurate, the pricing basis is defensible, and the NPV math checks. But the no-break 12-month commitment is not yet commercially safe for Ty because the proposal measures the case after he is already locked in.

Fix that by turning month 1 into visible execution on one live decision, while still using it to baseline months 2-12. If that fix lands, I would not block the send.
