# TTI — Ty Proposal: Fable Assessment of the Perplexity Report (9 Aug 2026)

## What this is, and why it exists

**What it is:** Fable's assessment of the Perplexity report [[perplexity-defensible-ea-benefits]] against [[tti-ty-engagement-proposal]] v3, answering Julian's two questions: are the five cost-reduction levers right, and are the measures of success right for a CFO. **This is a targeted assessment, not a third full adversarial round** - only the areas the Perplexity critique touched were examined (the lever table, the success measures, the NPV-of-delay claim). The commercial section, month-1 design, prior-proof claims and scope were not re-reviewed.

**Why it was created:** Julian commissioned the Perplexity report on 9 Aug over the same two concerns, and asked whether its suggestions should be adopted into the proposal before the Ty call on Mon 10 Aug.

**How it is used:** record of what was adopted, what was rejected, and why. Read before any future session reopens the lever framing or the measures question - the rejections here are reasoned, not omissions.

---

## Verdict

**The five levers stand.** Perplexity does not propose different levers; it restates the same five with sharper attribution (architect-owned inputs vs. business-owned outcomes). Lever selection is confirmed.

**The measures largely stand.** The proposal's posture - no benefit figure claimed, a breakeven hurdle instead, measures set against the month-1 baseline - already implements the report's own doctrine (metrics inside 90 days, no promised lagging outcomes, pilot before the big ask). The report was effectively reviewing an older framing than the one v3 landed on.

**Two findings adopted, three suggestion groups rejected.** Detail below.

## Findings adopted (both applied to the proposal 9 Aug, this session)

| # | Finding | Fix applied |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **"Direct NPV loss" was a free-floating quantitative claim** (Decision latency lever). An NPV-of-delay assertion with no discount rate or benefit stream is the one line in the lever table a numerate CFO can puncture - Perplexity flags exactly this. | Replaced with an anchor to the month-1 measurement: "a deferred decision defers the benefit stream behind it - month one puts a number on that." Same argument, no longer unquantified. |
| P2 | **The Strategic alignment lever ended at visibility, not decision.** "Can be killed or deferred" left open who kills - the report's "what if the map is built and nobody kills a project?" question. | Recommendation-logged framing added: a fund, defer or kill recommendation is logged per project; the investment decision stays with Ty. Matches the attribution posture the Procurement lever already used. |

One consequential addition to [[tti-ty-call-script]]: a new holding line for **"how would I measure you?"**, built from the report's leading/lagging split (named artefacts the architect controls, inspectable at quarter-end; savings tracked in TTI's numbers but not claimed as EA's). This question had no prepared answer anywhere in the call prep.

## Suggestions rejected, with reasons

1. **The four-Objective OKR framework (Objectives 1-4, 12 KRs).** A post-yes governance artefact, not sales content. Several KRs conflict with locked decisions: they centre on an ARB stood up in Q1, where the locked month-1 design is one live decision through the mechanism; the "90-day quick wins" duplicate what month 1 already delivers. Adopting would reopen structure that survived two adversarial rounds. **Retained as reference for contract/month-1 shaping if Ty says yes.**
2. **Third-party case-study dollar figures ($50-190M ISG, Thai Union, Hitachi).** Inserting them would contradict the proposal's strongest line - refusing unmeasured benefit figures - and invites sourcing scrutiny the Definition of Done in [[tti-ty-proposal]] explicitly guards against.
3. **Any structural rewrite.** The stopping condition is met, Ty makes contact 10 Aug, and every edit forces the three-file sync. Only edits that reduce risk on the call cleared the bar; P1 and P2 did, nothing else.
