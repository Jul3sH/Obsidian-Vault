# TTI Ty Engagement Proposal - Codex Review - 2026-08-08

## 1. Ty's 60-second reaction

This is much better. I now understand what you would do in the first month: take one stuck cross-region decision and force it into an owner, a standard, a ratifier and a date. That is a real mechanism, not just "architecture" as a belief system.

The fee is still defensible. The H1 numbers are handled intelligently, especially SG&A absorbing most of the gross-margin gain. The no-break commitment is still a big ask, but it no longer feels like I am paying you for a month to decide whether the role should exist.

My two reservations: first, the proof case is still unnamed, so the first month depends on me believing a suitable decision exists and can be selected quickly. Second, the procurement row says "Steve's own stated goal" on IT negotiating costs. Unless that is public, it makes me wonder how you know it. That is the one line I would not let travel.

That is the only FATAL issue I see. The 7 Aug fatal is substantially fixed. The remaining weaknesses are survivable if Julian is good on the call, especially with the BG Group prior-proof story ready.

Safe replacement:
