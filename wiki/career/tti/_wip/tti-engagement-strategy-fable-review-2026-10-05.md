# TTI Engagement Strategy: Fable review, 5 Oct 2026

## Purpose

Adversarial review of the open sub-item under [[tti-engagement-strategy]] point 3: raise the UK-based role now (tonight's call or a recap before 7 Oct), or hold it until Clodagh has answered an actual offer (point 6)? Tests both directions before the call.

## Verdict

**Hold. Do not propose a UK base tonight, and do not send a recap written for Ty to read.** Ask the three agreed questions. If Stephan names Ty's buy-in as the gate, ask one diagnostic instead of offering the fix: "Is it the Hong Kong seat that's the problem, or the hire itself?" That surfaces "it has to be Hong Kong", if it exists, without Julian putting a UK role on the record. Only if Stephan himself raises location does Julian say he is open to it, verbally.

**The one condition that flips this:** Stephan says 7 Oct decides yes or no and the stated objection is the Hong Kong seat. Then raise it on the call. Still no forwardable recap: in writing, through Stephan, to Ty, it is the 20 Aug route again.

## Reasoning

| Option | Gains | Risks | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raise now (call plus recap) | If location is the real objection, a yes on 7 Oct instead of a slow no | Horst hears "he doesn't want to move" and the only live offer dies (Julian's own repercussion; speculation about Horst); the recap re-asks the chain that said no, which the council says ends in a second no or a hollow yes; "working to the US-set strategy" gives up the charter positioning | [[tti-comms-log]] FIRST run; [[TTI-board-risk-Fable-Council]] root-cause check |
| Hold (point 6) | Keeps Horst's HK offer clean, the agreement in principle Julian is aiming for; the later ask is temporary, made with an offer in hand, for an external reason | If an HK hire is dead at Ty's level, Julian learns it weeks later; a location ask after an offer reopens one term | [[tti-engagement-strategy]] point 2; [[HK-Return-legal-position]] |
| Middle: diagnose, do not propose | Gets the fact Julian fears without owning a UK proposal; any trigger comes from Stephan (the 24 Aug rule) | Stephan may not answer plainly | [[tti-engagement-strategy]], 24 Aug point 4 |

**Where the record supports the strategy less than it assumes:**
- Nothing from TTI says location is the objection. Ty's 20 Aug reason was upscaling internal talent; Stephan on 25 Sep said nothing about where Julian sits. The timezone case is Julian's. Any reading of Ty's motive is speculation.
- Point 6 says "the UK-based option already on the table makes the flexibility an easier ask", but point 3 holds it back. The strategy contradicts itself; this review resolves it toward holding.
- Julian's fear is half right. "It has to be Hong Kong", said to a permanent UK role, does not answer a temporary work-from-UK bridge during a court case. What raising it now does close is the clean read on Horst's offer.
- "7 Oct decides it" is an assumption. The record has "with the team Oct 7th"; who the team is, is not known.

## What would change the answer

- Stephan states location as the objection (see Verdict).
- Horst, not Ty, says HK only. Then point 6 step 4 fails; raising it tonight would not have prevented that, and the ask belongs with Horst directly.
- Clodagh moves first (an order sought before any offer). The UK base becomes the main route, put to Horst, not through Ty.
