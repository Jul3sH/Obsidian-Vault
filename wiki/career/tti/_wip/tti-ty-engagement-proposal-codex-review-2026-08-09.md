## 1. Ty's 60-second reaction

This is now a serious proposal. I can see the shape: architecture capability, cost transparency, and Gen AI skills distribution, all tied back to cost, standards and decision speed. If you want me to commit for 12 months with no break clause, I need to believe those artefacts start operating change, not just describe it.

I would take the call. I would push hard on what I can hold you to by day 30, where you have done this before, and who is going to be irritated by this internally.

## 3. New Findings

None.

I do not see a current finding that should block the send under the stated stopping condition. The largest issue is month 1, but with Stephan sponsorship, a lowered fee, and three concrete artefacts in Julian's control, it is not fatal.

## 5. The Single Change That Most Improves the Odds of a Yes

Retitle and sharpen month 1 so it does not overclaim.

Suggested direction:

> **Month 1 starts delivery, not a study.** Thirty days, three inspectable outputs: the architecture capability baseline with decision-clearance times, the first categorised spend slice, and the Gen AI skills template with one candidate skill and its before-after measure defined.

That keeps every written commitment inside Julian's control, but removes the easy objection that "execution" means three documents.
