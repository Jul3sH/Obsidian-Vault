## Table manners (what NOT to do)

- **Do not accept at the table.** Warm agreement on direction, yes; commitment, no - written terms first (stand-down + reopen test pending).
- **Do not volunteer the custody situation as the driver.** It reads as desperation and hands over all leverage. The schooling/unwind frame carries every ask.
- **Do not accept a consulting-bridge reframe.** If it drifts to "start with 6 months and see", the answer is the pre-committed rule in your own words: you can only uproot Sophia again for something secure.
- **Nothing Justin-sourced** reaches Stephan or Horst (standing guardrail). The 3a holding lines apply if Stephan volunteers his rationalisations.
- **Get the next step named before leaving:** who sends what, by when. An open-ended "we'll sort it out" is the Kari/Ty pattern repeating.
