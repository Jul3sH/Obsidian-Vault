# Investment Holdings

## Purpose

A register of every fund Julian holds, with its identifiers and latest value. It exists so the investment portfolio can be reviewed against one accurate list.

Sections: Assumptions; Holdings (ISAs); MPF; Combined view (ISAs + MPF); Five-Year Performance, Charges and Tracker Comparison. The analysis and plan built on this register live in [[portfolio-rebalance-2026]].

> As of 10 Oct 2026: ISAs complete (£336,418.11). MPF added on assumed fund splits (see Assumptions). ISAs + MPF £457,263.

## Key Takeaways

- The ISIN is the key that identifies each fund. The name is there so a person can read it. SEDOL is inside a UK ISIN, and a platform fund code means nothing outside that platform.
- Account type (ISA, SIPP or GIA) and platform are recorded per fund because they decide tax treatment and switching options.
