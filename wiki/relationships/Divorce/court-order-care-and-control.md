# Court Order: Care and Control (FCMC 3390/2020)

**What is this?** The full record of the Hong Kong District Court orders of 2 November 2020 governing Sophia's custody, care and control: a summary of key terms followed by a word-for-word transcription of the sealed orders and the attached Parental Agreement.

## 2. Key Terms of the Orders (2 Nov 2020)

- **Custody, care and control:** joint, to both parents.
- **Removal from Hong Kong (the operative clause):** the child is not to be removed from Hong Kong without leave until age 18, *provided that* if either parent gives a general written undertaking to the court to return the child to Hong Kong when called upon, that parent may remove her from Hong Kong for any period specified in the written consent of the other parent.
- **Financial support:** each parent undertook to contribute HK$12,500 per month into a joint account for Sophia's expenses.
- **Parental Agreement of 28 August 2020** attached to and forming part of the order: equal-time parenting schedule, alternating holidays, DBIS schooling, joint decision-making, and mediation before any motion to modify.
- **Address changes:** anyone under a payment order must notify the other party of an address change within 14 days (Important Notice on the sealed orders).

Source PDFs (scans of the sealed orders and Parental Agreement): "2020-11-03 Care and control court orders part 1", "part 2", and cover letter.

## 6. Verbatim: Parental Agreement of 28 August 2020

Transcribed word for word from the signed copy attached to the care and control order (case number handwritten: FCMC 3390/2020; each page initialled by both parties). Original typographical errors are preserved.

> Parental Agreement, C and J
>
> PARENTAL AGREEMENT OF
>
> **Julian Hart – The Petitoer, The Father**
> **And**
> **Clodagh O'Connor also known as Clodagh Hart - the Respondent, the Mother**
>
> MARITAL BACKGROUND
>
> 1. The **Mother** is now a resident of 2B Woodland Court, Parkvale Village, Discovery Bay, Lantau, NT, Hong Kong;
> 2. The **Father** is now a resident of 12D Sunrise, 3 Parkridge Crescent, Discovery Bay, Lantau, NT, Hong Kong;
> 3. The **Father** and the **Mother** being hereinafter together referred as "The Parents".
> 4. The Parents were lawfully joined in marriage on 16th July 2011 at St John The Baptist Church, Crooke in the Diocese of Waterford & Lismore, Ireland;
> 5. There is one child of the marriage, namely: Sophia Isabelle Hart, a girl, born on 25 May 2014, referred as "Sophia", who lives in two residences, the Father's home, and the Mother's home.
> 6. The Parents were separated on or about 8th September, 2018.
>
> GENERAL CONCEPT
>
> 1. It is the Parents intention to seek an Agreement following their separation and agree to be cooperative for the best interests of Sophia. The Parents agree to solve in an amicable way any disagreement that may hereafter be raised by either or both of them regarding this Agreement.
> 2. The Parents therefore jointly decided to use the services of a mediator to reasonably resolve any differences and understand that this cooperation requires the acceptance of mutual responsibilities as well as individual and mutual rights insofar as Sophia concerned, and agree to put Sophia needs ahead of their own.
> 3. The Parents agree to establish two homes for Sophia, and will continue to provide them until she reaches the age of 21 and complete full education.
> 4. The Parents acknowledge that it is Sophia's right to maintain reasonable and equal access/contact with each parent and they will ensure that the arrangements made for spending the respective parenting time with Sophia will take into account each Parent's work schedule as well as Sophia's needs and school calendar.
>
> PARENTING PLAN
>
> 1. The Parents agree to Joint Custody, Care and Control.
>
> **Education**
>
> 2. It is agreed that Sophia, who is currently in Early Year 1 in DBIS school in Discovery Bay, Hong Kong, will spend year 2 to year 13 in DBIS school in Discovery/
> 3. This schooling plan can be modified with the express written consent of both parents.
> 4. The Parents obligations for further education are conditioned upon the following:
>    a) Sophia have at that time the desire and aptitude for a college/university education;
>    b) The college/university is limited to four consecutive years after graduation from high school; however, the time for both shall be extended in case of serious illness, and the time for education if she seeks to study Medicine or a masters.
>    c) The decisions affecting the education of Sophia, including the choice of college/university, shall be made jointly by the parents and shall consider the expressed preference of Sophia, but neither parent shall unreasonably withhold his or her consent to the expressed preference of the child;
>
> **Parenting Time Schooldays and weekdays**
>
> 5. The Parents agreed that Sophia will spend equal time with each one of them as described in the following table:
>
> |  | Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday | Friday | Saturday | Sunday |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | week 1 | J | J | C | J | C | J | C |
> | week 2 | C | C | J | C | J | C | J |
> | week 3 | J | J | C | J | C | J | C |
> | week 4 | C | C | J | C | J | C | J |
>
> 6. the Parents agreed to adopt flexibility attitude towards the parenting schedule described in paragraph 5 above, taking into consideration their work commitments.
> 7. The parent who has Sophia is responsible for delivering her, either to the other parent who is supposed to have her, or to the event or activity she is supposed to attend, or to the helper. The formal time for handover is 6pm each day. This will be after school on a typical weekday, or after daytime activity at the weekend.
>
> **Holidays and School Breaks**
>
> 8. It is agreed that Holidays and School Breaks will be split equally between the parents on an alternate basis.
> 9. As for main holidays (Christmas, Gregorian New Year, Chinese New Year, Easter, mid-term breaks and summer holiday) the parents agreed to maintain equality regarding time spent with Sophia on an alternate basis as follow:
>    a) The Mother will have time with Sophia on Xmas every <u>even</u> year while the Father will have time with Sophia on CNY and Easter every <u>even</u> year, starting 2020. This is for the full duration of the school holidays which is currently three weeks for Christmas, two weeks for Easter, and a week for CNY
>    b) The Father will have time with Sophia on Xmas every odd year while the Mother ill have time with Sophia on CNY and Easter every odd year, starting 2021. This is also for the full duration of the school holidays which is currently three weeks for Christmas, two weeks for Easter, and a week for CNY
>    c) Both parents are entitled to two weeks summer holiday
>    If both parents are in Hong Kong during the holiday, then they can choose to continue the normal routine, however, it will not influence another holiday period.
> 10. In case of long weekends, or in the event of sporadic public holidays that fall adjacent to a weekend that either one of the parent is responsible for, the Parent that spend the weekend with Sophia has the right to extend his/her weekend with Sophia to include the day(s) before or the day(s) after the visit's weekend (i.e., the holiday days); this should be done with prior coordination and consent between the parents.
> 11. Each parent is permitted to change the weekend schedule once each term, entitling them to keep Sophia the entire weekend (Friday evening until Monday morning). In response, the other parent is then entitled (but does not have to exercise the right) to also keep Sophia for the entire weekend during the term.

>
> 12. Sophia will be with her mother on Mother's Day and with her father on Father's Day;
> 13. Sophia will be with her mother on her birthday and with her father on his birthday;
> 14. Either parent may travel outside of Hong Kong with Sophia during his/her parenting time and should get the other parent Consent in writing in advance as follow:
>     a) For long overseas trip over Xmas/CNY/Easter/Summer holiday: at least 3 months in advance
>     b) For any other trip: at least 7 days in advance
>     In case of such travel, the parent must notify the other parent and provide him/her with telephone numbers at which the traveling parent and Sophia can be reached in the event of an emergency;
> 15. In all cases both parents will make all efforts to ensure all passports of Sophia of the marriage are readily available both for travel and as needed otherwise for issuance of visas and other necessary formalities.

> **Temporary Variance From the Schedule**
>
> 16. Each parent shall advise the other parent directly by telephone and/or in writing as soon as said parent becomes aware of a need for a temporary variance from the parenting schedule. All communications are to be directed toward the other parent, and not through Sophia;
> 17. The Parents keep one another informed as to work schedules, vacation plans, or other information that might impact upon the need for, and ability to accommodate, a request for a temporary variance from the parenting schedule.
>
> **Decision-Making Rights and Responsibilities**
>
> 18. Each parent should be notified of any occurrence involving Sophia of which a parent would normally be notified, including but not limited to disciplinary action, medical emergencies, illness, educational difficulties, awards, or any other such occurrences;
> 19. Each parent shall have equal access to the educational, medical, and dental information of Sophia of the marriage and the Mother further undertake to provide all information from schools; extra-curricular programs; events, health, dental, and vision care providers; and any and all other agencies or entities involved in Sophia's life.
> 20. The Parents undertake to keep one another informed of all information of which we each become aware regarding educational, extra-curricular, medical, dental, or other events, activities or issues regarding Sophia.
> 21. The Parents undertake to respect each other, each other's homes and rules, and neither parent shall dictate the day to day events in the other parent's household, but each parent shall cooperate to provide Sophia with a consistent, caring, nurturing, safe environment;
> 22. In the event that the Parents cannot agree on major decisions relating to the education, medical, and dental care of Sophia, or on any aspect of this parenting plan, they shall participate in up to/at least five hours of mediation with a jointly selected family mediator prior to filing any motion to modify this parenting plan. > 23. The Parents shall keep each other informed of current home and work telephone numbers and addresses.

An application to the Court is to be made by consent pursuant to this Agreement and the Courts of Hong Kong shall have exclusive jurisdiction over this Agreement at that time and in the future, even if the Courts of one or more other legal jurisdictions also have jurisdiction to entertain the application, as the parents agree that they hereby submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Courts of Hong Kong to deal with such application, this Agreement, and all matters pertaining to it, now and in the future.

## Key Takeaways

- The 2 Nov 2020 HK orders give joint custody, care and control, and bar removal from Hong Kong without leave or the clause 1 consent mechanism (written undertaking to return plus the other parent's written consent for a specified period).
- The Parental Agreement of 28 Aug 2020 forms part of the order and gives the Hong Kong courts exclusive jurisdiction over it.
- Each parent undertook HK$12,500 per month into the joint account for Sophia's expenses.
- The retrospective notification of Sophia's Sept 2026 removal is drafted in [[removal-notification-letter]].
