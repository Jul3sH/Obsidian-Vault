# Worcestershire Family Support Links

## Purpose
These are the family wellbeing, parenting and family-law services that Worcestershire County Council's Family Connect sent Julian on 24 Sep 2026, after Worcestershire Police contacted them. They are kept here so the contacts are easy to find when support or advice is needed.

**Source:** Kerry Ashmore, Initial Screening and Contact Officer, Family Connect (Family Front Door), Worcestershire County Council. Tel: 01905 822666, option 2. Email: FamilyConnect@worcestershire.gov.uk

## Key Takeaways
- **Nearest local help:** Sunshine Family Hub in Malvern (01684 577442) is the one-stop shop for families.
- **Free legal advice:** Child Law Advice Line (0300 330 5480) covers child, family and education law in England.
- **Young people's mental health (11 to 25):** YourSpace has a hub in Malvern. Under-16s need a parent or professional referral and parental consent. Kooth offers anonymous online support.
- **Co-parenting tools:** Cafcass Parenting Plan, Family Mediation, child contact centres, and co-parenting apps.

## Legal and court

| Service | What it offers | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| Child Law Advice Line (Coram Children's Legal Centre) | Free legal advice and information on child, family and education law for parents, carers and young people in England | Tel: 0300 330 5480 · childlawadvice.org.uk |
| Cafcass | Advises the family courts on children's welfare and best interests. Its Parenting Plan page covers "planning together for children", a course that promotes cooperative parenting | https://www.cafcass.gov.uk/ |
| Advicenow | Advice and downloadable guides on going to family court, child arrangements, divorce and finance | https://www.advicenow.org.uk/family-court |
| Family Mediation Council | Family mediation | www.familymediationcouncil.org.uk |
