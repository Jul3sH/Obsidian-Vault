# Techtronic Covid Essay Ideas

## Task Description
Produce a set of research-based idea/argument threads on how Covid-19 changed ways of working (during the pandemic and since), using Techtronic Industries as a case study, for Justin's son to research and write up in his own words.

## Completion Criteria
1. A set of idea/argument threads delivered covering Covid's impact on ways of working, both during the pandemic and how it shaped current practice
2. A Techtronic Industries case-study angle woven into at least one thread, grounded only in publicly available information
3. Ideas delivered as prompts/research angles/arguments, not drafted essay prose

## Definition of Done
A written set of idea/argument threads exists, each with enough of a research pointer that the student can independently research and write the essay himself. Includes a dedicated Techtronic angle.

## Load-Bearing Assumptions
- **Assumption:** the Techtronic case-study material can be sourced from public information only, without drawing on Julian's personal/professional relationship with Justin or his own knowledge as a TTI job candidate.
  - **Cheap test:** before using any TTI fact, confirm it is findable via a public source (news coverage, TTI's own investor/PR materials) rather than something known only through the friendship or the interview process.

## Prompt Zero
*Written 2026-08-07 by Julian, in his own words. Read this in full before working on this deliverable.*

1. **Trying to accomplish:** Produce a set of essay ideas that enables Justin's son to get started on a report when he's currently a little bit lost in terms of how to get on with it, because it's his first report.

2. **Why it matters / stakes:** The undergrad has never had to do a piece of work like this and doesn't know how to leverage AI for research. This will help him do a good job and learn the process of essay writing using AI for research.

3. **Done looks like:** A written set of idea/argument threads, each with enough of a research pointer that he can independently research and write the essay himself. Covers Covid's impact on ways of working both during the pandemic and since. Includes a Techtronic angle. Ideas, not drafted prose.

4. **Wrong looks like:** Verbose. The ideas need to be short, succinct, and easy to reconstruct into narratives. Also wrong: dropping a valid point just because there's no Techtronic fact to hang on it. If we have a fact, we use it; if we don't, we don't necessarily drop the point.

5. **What I know that isn't written down:** The report needs to be balanced across People, Process, and Tools. People: the cultural shift, getting used to working from home and remote meetings instead of face-to-face. Process: supply chain and other processes that had to fundamentally change because of logistical issues. Tools: remote login, and workloads moving to the cloud once they realised you don't want to backhaul all that remote traffic into the data centres. Public TTI sources are already collected in `TTI covid report/` (annual + interim reports 2020, ten CNBC transcripts 2020-2024, 2020 press release, Home Depot and Walmart earnings as retail context).

6. **The pieces:** Analyse the source folder first, then discuss what angles the available information actually supports, then produce the ideas. Reverse-engineering from what we have, not from a pre-set structure. Coverage across People / Process / Tools.

7. **The hard part:** Working together iteratively to decide which ideas to keep.
