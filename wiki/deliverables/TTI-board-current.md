## Purpose

A snapshot of who sits on TTI's board today and how the directors split between the old Hong Kong group and the US leadership around CEO Steven Richman. It exists so Julian can see where board influence sits when judging the Horst-mandated role.

Summary first; then the director table, the three groupings, and where control sits.

# Techtronic Industries (TTI): Board of Directors Analysis

As at 2 October 2026.

## Key Takeaways

- TTI's board has 13 directors: 5 Group Executive Directors and 8 Independent Non-executive Directors ([TTI corporate information](https://www.ttigroup.com/investors/shareholder-services/corporate-information)).
- Using the "old school Hong Kong" definition (with Horst Pudwill on the journey since he built the company in the mid-80s), the core is 2 to 5 directors, depending on how strictly you count.
- Only 3 to 4 directors line up with CEO Steven Richman and the American leadership. The rest are international professionals who don't fit either group.
- This split is based on backgrounds and appointment timing. The independent directors are formally independent, and nothing public shows any personal loyalty either way.

## Old-School Hong Kong (2 to 5)

- **Strict definition, 2:** Horst Pudwill (1985) and Frank Chan (1991, close enough).
- **Wider Pudwill and Hong Kong circle, 5:** add Stephan Pudwill (family), Camille Jojo (Hong Kong legal establishment) and Peter Sullivan (Hong Kong banking establishment, on the board 18 years).

## US and Richman-Aligned (3, maybe 4)

- **Steven Richman:** the Milwaukee/American operating leadership.
- **Robert Getz:** New York private equity. Made Lead Independent Director in March 2025, the year after Richman became CEO.
- **Virginia Wilmerding:** US-based, with a US governance and investor-relations focus.
- **Stephen Wong (possible fourth):** a Silicon Valley venture capitalist, but his career is with Goldman in Hong Kong. Joined two months after Richman joined the board.

## Neither Group (4 to 5)

Hans-Gerd Hesse, Caroline Kracht and Andrew Roberts are European professionals who spent much of their careers in Hong Kong or Asia. Karen Ng is Hong Kong-based. She and Frank Chan both sit on the Gold Peak board, which points slightly towards the local network.

## Where Control Sits

Horst still holds the key levers:

- **Shares:** about 21.86% of the company.
- **Board appointments:** he chairs the Nomination Committee, so he controls who joins the board.
- **Tenure:** the board's three longest-serving members (39, 35 and 34 years, as of March 2026) were all Hong Kong-era executives: Horst, Patrick Chan and Frank Chan.

The US influence is growing through the CEO role and the Lead Independent Director, but it's still a minority on the board ([TTI 2025 Annual Report](https://doc.irasia.com/listco/hk/techtronic/annual/2025/ar2025.pdf)).
