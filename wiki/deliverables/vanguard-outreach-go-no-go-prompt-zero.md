# Prompt Zero - Vanguard Outreach Go/No-Go

*Written 2026-08-05 by Julian, in his own words, via `/prompt-zero`. Read this in full before working on [[vanguard-outreach-go-no-go]]. It outranks any later reading of the request.*

*Framework: the Seven Questions, by Nate B Jones.*

---

**1. What am I actually trying to accomplish?**

A plan that sets out the legal position clearly enough for Adam to make an informed choice, including knowingly accepting some risk of unlawfulness, and that is structured so any resulting exposure sits with him rather than me, with my role limited to advising rather than executing.

**2. Why does this matter?**

A favour to a friend. No commercial upside for me. If I don't do it, the cost falls on Adam, who is struggling and needs the business. The stakes are asymmetric: my upside is zero and my downside is the only real legal tail risk in the picture. The partnership may continue through other legal routes such as LinkedIn advertising, but this piece is a favour and I'm going to transfer the risk.

**3. What does "done" look like?**

Three pages, high level, detail deferred.

- **p1** - the HK regulations that apply to a B2C outreach campaign, including SFC where relevant even if assumption-based, with the assumptions flagged.
- **p2** - key risks as "if we do this, then that could happen", each with one-line candidate mitigations aimed at transferring my risk to Adam.
- **p3** - a succinct recommended action plan.

Read by me first, to hold in my head and take into a conversation with Adam about what to do next.

**4. What does "wrong" look like?**

Verbosity. Succinct, not a doctorate. A lot of these outputs are so detailed my head explodes, and I'm the one who has to make the decision, so if I can't hold it I can't use it. One-liners on p2. Detail belongs in later artefacts.

**5. What do I already know that I haven't written down?**

- Adam sells UK investment property to Hong Kong individuals: direct residential, commercial, and commercial variants such as assisted living. It varies by project.
- He is the data owner; I would be the agent. Knowingly supplying lists implicates me; pure execution may not (unverified, see the assumption table in the enabler).
- Two regimes: **UEMO** covers how you do it, **PDPO** covers the data. Research document supplied at [[hk-b2c-outreach-uemo-pdpo]].
- **List sources:** existing contacts via Adam's partner, a 25% shareholder, possibly originating in other business lines including whisky sales and investments; contacts bought through a previous cold outreach campaign; scraped and enriched to be covered too, as likely future sources.
- **Already in my name and live:** two email domains and the SalesHandy subscription currently warming them. Nothing has been sent yet.
- Adam asked me to run it. I told him I won't take risks until I've looked at it.

**6. What are the pieces?**

One piece. Produce the three-page report, then have the conversation with Adam. Nothing else until that conversation has happened.

**7. What's the hard part?**

If Adam gives me explicit instruction to run it, states that he provided the lists, and takes full responsibility, can I execute it using my own SalesHandy outreach account without being legally implicated?

And more generally: how sound are any of the mitigations? Reading the report may show I need additional views, an LLM Council, or further research. High level is what I want at this stage.
