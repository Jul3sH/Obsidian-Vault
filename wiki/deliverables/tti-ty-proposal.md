# TTI — Ty Proposal (12-month engagement)

## Task Description
Write the proposal Julian takes Ty Staviski through on Monday 10 Aug: what he would do, the scope, a 12-month structure whose first month is a discovery audit that sets the scope for months 2-12, and the compensation figure.

## Completion Criteria
1. Proposal document exists covering scope, the 12-month structure with month 1 as the discovery audit that sets months 2-12, and the compensation figure (HK$208,418/month, US$320,644/yr).
2. The pricing basis is stated or ready to state on request — last base + employer voluntary pension +25%, full cost recovery, no risk or profit loading.
3. Kari's insight on TTI's actual needs is either incorporated into the scope section, or explicitly recorded in this file as not obtained before the call.
4. The contradiction in [[independent-consulting-pricing]] is resolved: the "Pricing posture" section (open at 1.5x / ~HK$3.12M) is reconciled with the 6 Aug DECIDED block (HK$2.5M), so a single instruction stands.

## Definition of Done
A proposal exists that Ty can act on without a follow-up clarification, giving him the term and the number Stephan asked for while being honest that the detailed scope is not yet knowable. Month 1 is a priced discovery audit with named outputs and a pre-agreed gate; months 2-12 are shaped by what it finds. The compensation figure is stated with a basis that survives a finance review, and no claim in the document is one Julian would have to walk back under questioning.

## Load-Bearing Assumptions

**1. Ty will accept scope being set in month 1 rather than named up front.**
If false, the audit-first structure reads as vagueness rather than rigour, and the proposal fails at exactly the point it is trying to be honest. Cheap test: Kari — he understands TTI's needs, sponsored the 12-month frame himself on 24 Jul, and has already been messaged. Ask directly whether Ty will wear a discovery-first structure. Fallback if no answer arrives: name two or three concrete month-1 outputs so the structure is specific even where the scope is not.

**2. This has to be clear-cut enough for Ty to decide inside ~24 hours** (Julian's own framing).
If false, the proposal's job changes from closing to creating a dated next step, and it should be written differently. Cheap test: Julian checks with Stephan on Monday whether Ty can actually move this forward, before the call rather than after it. This test is already planned and costs one message.

**3. A consulting contract gives Julian the right to work in Hong Kong.**
Flagged by the 6 Aug [[llm-council]] and surviving every advisor until peer review: a consulting contract is not HK employment sponsorship. If this is false, no rate and no scope matters, and the whole engagement shape is wrong. Cheap test: Kari, who holds HK tax and IT influence and is the correct person to answer it. Cost: one question on a channel already open.

## Payoff Test
- **Payoff:** Commercial. Named buyer is TTI (Ty Staviski as the decision-maker, Stephan as the channel). The artefact they pay for is a 12-month engagement at US$320,644.
- **Silence test:** Pass. The payoff is income and remaining in Hong Kong; it does not depend on anyone knowing about the work.
- **Good enough when:** an independent adversarial pass (skeptical Ty / finance lens, run by a model other than the one that drafted it) returns no FATAL findings, and every claim in the document is one Julian can evidence if Ty pushes. SERIOUS and MINOR findings do not block the send.

## Constraints
- **Keep the fee off anything that travels.** Per [[independent-consulting-pricing]], a fee on a leave-behind anchors low and invites scrutiny — and Tony Chung is the documented reason that matters here. The figure goes in the proposal to Ty or is said aloud, not into any artefact that routes onward to procurement.
- **Establish the format before building for it.** Stephan said Ty will "reach out" — if that is a phone call rather than a scheduled video meeting, the written proposal is the load-bearing artefact and a deck is dead weight.
- **Price both terms.** Stephan asked for 6 *or* 12 months. A single number concedes the shorter option free (6-month rate: HK$218,252/month). Named as a council blind spot on 6 Aug.
