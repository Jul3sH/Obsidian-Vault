# MM: Commitment-Stalling Bias

This card names the bias of completing the analysis but letting the commit point recede. Confirmed with verified quoted evidence across multiple independent instances (May-Aug 2026) during [[bias-history-review]], promoting the memory-only correction (`feedback-commitment-forcing`) to a card. The detail article is [[commitment-avoidance]]; the countermeasure card is [[mm-commit-with-a-forcing-function]].

**One-liner:** Analysis complete is not decided - the commit point recedes until something external forces it.

**Reach for it when:** the analysis is done but the choice is not written; or a draft is finished but gathering "one more" review gate before sending; or a decision waits on an unvalidated external signal.

## Key Takeaways

- The stall wears working clothes: extra review gates, model upgrades before sending, micro-revision loops, waiting for a third party who has not committed to reply.
- The likely mechanism is options-preservation: an open decision keeps all options alive, and closing it kills all but one. The 3 Jul 2026 introspection named it directly ("it's about not wanting to give up the feeling of options and of abundance").
- The stall is not more analysis - by this point analysis adds nothing. It is the avoidance of loss.

## Principles

- **A decision is made when it is written and dated, not when the analysis supports it.**
- **Waiting on an external signal is a decision to stall unless that signal has a date.**
- **The feeling of open options is worth nothing if the options are never exercised.**

## Guidelines

- Apply [[mm-commit-with-a-forcing-function]]: a deadline, a booking, or a written choice ends analysis.
- At a send point, name what the next review round could change; if nothing, send.
- If waiting on a person: set the date on which waiting ends and what happens then.

## Limitations

- A genuine pending fact with a known arrival date is a legitimate reason to schedule (not stall) a decision.
- Once a decision is locked, this card hands over to [[mm-decision-reopening-bias]] - stalling is pre-commitment, reopening is post.
