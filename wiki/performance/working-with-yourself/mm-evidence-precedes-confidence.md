## Purpose

The Evidence Precedes Confidence mental model: confidence comes from evidence that you can do the thing, so the way to build it is to produce that evidence, not to wait for the feeling. It replaces `mm-competence-as-fuel` and its detail article `competence-as-fuel` under a clearer name.

# MM: Evidence Precedes Confidence

**One-liner:** Confidence follows evidence. Deliver first, and let confidence follow.

**Reach for it when:** you're waiting to feel confident, ready or good enough before acting.

## Key Takeaways

- Confidence isn't something you summon. It's what you feel once there's evidence you can do the thing.
- The strongest evidence is a real result: something delivered, used, or noticed by others.
- Evidence you already have counts. Your habit is to focus on what hasn't been achieved yet; looking at what has is what rebuilds confidence.
- Genuinely new, risky action that gets a result shifts how you see yourself. Safe, small adjustments don't.
- Building, analysing and learning work produces evidence steadily, and it's a reliable source of energy for you (likely amplified by ADHD hyperfixation). It isn't a distraction from real work.
- Without fresh evidence, self-belief erodes quietly. Weeks away from work that produces results is a warning sign, not a neutral gap.

## Principles

- **Evidence first, confidence second.** Waiting to feel confident before acting reverses the order.
- **Results beat smartness.** Comparing yourself to smarter or more eloquent people freezes you. Results are what get noticed.
- **Risk plus results shifts identity.** New behaviour that gets a real result changes self-image; incremental tweaks don't.
- **Count what you've already got.** Acknowledged achievements are evidence too.

## Guidelines

| Situation | Fix |
|-----------|-----|
| **Confidence low before a decision or task** | Find the smallest real delivery that would produce evidence, and do that. |
| **Stalled by comparison with others** | Take the next concrete action instead of trying to out-think the comparison. |
| **Down about goals not moving** | List what you've actually achieved, and ask what you want acknowledged for. |
| **Weeks without results-producing work** | Treat it as a leading indicator of self-esteem drift. Schedule building or learning time explicitly; don't leave it to spare cycles. |

## Limitations

- It explains where confidence comes from, not what's worth delivering. That's a prioritisation question.
- It doesn't help with taste or judgement calls where no outside result will come back.
- Getting started despite fear is a separate card: [[mm-start-to-start]].
