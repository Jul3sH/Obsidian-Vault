# MM: Decision-Reopening Bias

This card names the bias of emotionally reopening decisions already locked. It is bias #1 in the [[commitment-lock-protocol]]'s register and the reason the commitment-guard skill exists; the [[bias-history-review]] scan verdict was "confirmed but already managed" - the strongest instances (5 Jul, 7 Jul, 18 Aug 2026) track the definition cleanly, while several others proved to be legitimate updating on genuinely new facts. This card formalises the pattern; the machinery that defends against it already exists.

**One-liner:** The feeling that follows a locked decision is forecast, not evidence.

**Reach for it when:** doubt returns after a locked decision - especially within 72 hours of the lock, or within 48 hours of a warm conversation.

## Key Takeaways

- The reopening urge follows contact and emotion, not new facts ("I have woken up panicking about my decision... I think this is going to happen every morning", 7 Jul 2026).
- The evidence base is one long saga (the UK move) reopened repeatedly - which is exactly how the bias presents: the same decision, re-argued.
- Legitimate reversal exists and has a lawful route: a genuinely new material fact, run through the reopen test in a scheduled session - never an on-the-spot U-turn.

## Principles

- **A wobble is information about who you spoke to, not about the decision.**
- **New feeling is not new fact.** Only a new fact can reopen.
- **Staying decided is the skill; deciding was the easy part.**

## Guidelines

- Run the F-N-M-T reopen test and the 60-second wobble drill from [[commitment-lock-protocol]].
- 48-hour stand-down after any warm contact that prompted the wobble; 72-hour no-action window after a branch fires.
- Re-read the decision's anchor document instead of re-arguing from inside the feeling.

## Limitations

- This card fires only on decisions the journal marks committed or locked. Open decisions belong to [[mm-commitment-stalling-bias]].
- A pre-registered branch trigger firing is not a reopen - it is the standing decision executing.
