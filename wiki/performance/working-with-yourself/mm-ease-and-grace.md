# MM: Ease and Grace

This is the Ease and Grace mental model: whether the composed, unhurried leadership
persona is showing up by decision or by mood. It exists because the pattern keeps
failing the same way, project slips into whichever state the day happens to produce,
not the one that was decided on in advance. Read it before a meeting, a bad mood, or
a drink puts the built persona at risk. The full detail lives in
[[calm-composed-leadership-persona]] and [[eq-integrity-and-ease-of-grace]].

**One-liner:** The composed persona is a decision made in advance, not a mood.

**Reach for it when:** you notice mood, tiredness, frustration, or circumstance
starting to dictate how you're showing up, rather than the persona you decided on.

## Key Takeaways

- Composure is chosen before the situation demands it, not improvised in the moment.
- Leadership behaviour is a responsibility in every mindset, not just the good ones.
- Cantankerous, huffing, over-familiar, or dramatic behaviour is weakness wearing a
  disguise, not strength.
- Unresolved disputes and open loops drain performance whether or not you notice.
- Ease and grace is a context you switch into, not a personality trait you either
  have or don't.

## Principles

- **Decide the persona in advance.** Visualise calm before the situation, don't
  react your way into it.
- **Lead in every mindset**, not only the peak ones. Sitting back because you're
  not feeling it is still a choice, and the wrong one.
- **Cantankerous, huffing, and over-familiar behaviour all read as weakness**,
  however justified they feel in the moment.
- **Bring nothing into interactions.** Accumulated history and grievance distort
  them; meet people and situations fresh.
- **Clear the mess.** Unresolved disputes and incomplete commitments occupy mental
  bandwidth even when you're not consciously thinking about them.
- **The context is chosen, not found.** Shifting from "life is tough and unfair" to
  "ease and grace" changes the behaviour that follows from it.

## Guidelines

- Before a meeting or interaction that could go either way, name the persona you're
  choosing, not just the outcome you want.
- A joke, a big grin, or over-familiarity all cost more credibility than they earn;
  reserve them for rooms where rapport is already established.
- When restraint feels costly in the moment, it is still cheaper than the day you'd
  otherwise have.
- Treat an unresolved dispute as a performance issue, not just a relationship one;
  resolve it or become genuinely complete with it.

## Limitations

This model governs how you show up, not what you decide or say. It doesn't replace
judgement about when to be genuinely vulnerable or transparent, being composed is not
the same as being closed. It also isn't a substitute for actually admitting mistakes
or fixing them, a graceful mistake still needs fixing.
