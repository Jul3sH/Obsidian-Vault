# MM: Certainty-Spike Bias

This card names the bias of treating a spike of total conviction as a green light when it is actually a warning sign. Documented as bias #3 in the [[commitment-lock-protocol]] register: "100% convinced" arriving from a man whose honest analytical outputs are ranges (30-50%).

**One-liner:** From someone whose honest outputs are ranges, "100% convinced" is a warning light, not a green one.

**Reach for it when:** feeling completely sure - especially suddenly, and especially after a warm contact or a good day.

## Key Takeaways

- Genuine analysis produces ranges and residual doubts. Total certainty is an emotional state wearing analytical clothes.
- The spike usually has a traceable trigger (a conversation, a vivid image, relief at escaping ambivalence) - find it before trusting it.
- The correct response is not to reverse the lean but to slow it: certainty this high must survive an anchor re-read intact.

## Principles

- **Self-conviction above ~90% is a signal to check, not to act.**
- **The strength of a feeling is not the strength of the case.**
- **Certainty that cannot name its evidence is mood.**

## Guidelines

- On a certainty spike: mandatory anchor-document re-read before any action or announcement ([[commitment-lock-protocol]]).
- Ask: what changed since the last honest range? If nothing, the range still stands.
- Check the trigger against [[mm-recency-bias]] - the two usually travel together.

## Limitations

- Some questions genuinely have near-certain answers (arithmetic, dated facts). The card applies to judgement calls where honest analysis previously produced a range.
