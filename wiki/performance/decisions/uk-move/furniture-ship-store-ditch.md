# Furniture: Ship, Store, or Ditch

This decides what happens to the big furniture in the HK flat (sofa, cupboards, coffee and dining tables) once Julian leaves: shipped to the UK, put into HK storage, kept in place under a furnished let, or sold/given away. It exists because the two-stage shipment split (3a.2) parks this decision out of the critical path, but it still needs an answer before the flat's letting type is finalised. Julian and Claude read and update it as the shipping-survey quotes and storage/replacement costs come in.

## The decision

Not yet made. Blocked on data (see below), not on judgment - the rule that will settle it is already fixed.

## Options, in Julian's preference order

1. **Rent the flat furnished** - furniture stays in use, zero cost, preserved for a possible return.
2. **HK storage against a return** - but 4-6 years of fees accumulate.
3. **Ship to UK** - not essential at Mum's but cost-effective if shipping < cumulative storage; possibly re-ship on a return.
4. **Give away / sell** - forced where both shipping and storage exceed replacement cost. The large sofa is the likely candidate here.

## Decision rule

**Never pay more to ship or store an item than it costs to replace.** This is a fixed comparison, not a judgment call - once the three inputs below exist, the rule mechanically picks the option per item.

## Blocked on

- Per-item shipping quotes
- HK storage fees over a 4-6yr horizon
- UK replacement costs
- Source: the 3a.2 shipping-surveyor visits are the intended input for the shipping and HK-storage figures; furniture-shipping and HK-storage pricing need adding to that survey agenda.
- Also gated by 3a.1: the flat's letting type (furnished vs unfurnished) decides which items are even in scope - furnished removes the big furniture from the shipment question entirely. No date yet for that answer; it may land after Julian's 15 Sept departure.

## Status

**2026-08-31: open, no data yet.** Julian's preference order above stands as the working lean until the TCO inputs arrive. Nothing here changes the Stage 1 shipment (clothes, personal items, TV/monitor), which proceeds regardless.
