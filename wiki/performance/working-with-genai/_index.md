The fourth "working with" folder, alongside [[performance/working-with-yourself/_index|Working With Yourself]] and [[performance/working-with-others/_index|Working With Others]]. Not part of Josh Kaufman's Personal MBA, which predates the problem, but the same shape: a category of relationship that has its own failure modes and its own disciplines.

Scope is the **concepts** - attention, trust, judgement, verification, and how to decide what to hand over. Locked-down process for Julian's own AI operating layer is [[ai-os/taxonomy|AI OS]]; generic tool and platform reference is [[technology/_index|Technology]]. The test: a principle that would hold for anyone working with generative AI belongs here, however it happens to be wired up in his setup.
