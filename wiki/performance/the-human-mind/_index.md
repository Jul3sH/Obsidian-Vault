Based on Chapter 6 of Josh Kaufman's The Personal MBA.

## What belongs here

Models and articles about how the mind itself works: biases, perception, and reasoning failures that apply to any decision in any domain. If the content is about managing yourself (time, energy, emotion), it belongs in [[performance/working-with-yourself/_index|Working With Yourself]]; if it is about other people, [[performance/working-with-others/_index|Working With Others]].
