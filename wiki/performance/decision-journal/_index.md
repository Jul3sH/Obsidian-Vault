# Decision Journal

**What belongs here:** real decisions and the live decision system built to make and defend them: the journal convention, the register, one `dec-*.md` file per decision, the commitment-lock protocol, the decision-maker profile, and decision-specific workspaces such as `uk-move/`. Reference knowledge about HOW to decide (frameworks, mental models) belongs in [[decision-frameworks/_index|Decision Frameworks]] instead.

## Convention and system
- [[decision-journal|Decision Journal]] - the logging convention and entry template
- [[decisions-register|Decisions Register]] - single table, one row per decision, for spotting patterns across decisions
- [[commitment-lock-protocol|Commitment-Lock Protocol]] - bias register and the F-N-M-T reopen test that defends a committed decision against the emotional U-turn
- [[decision-maker-profile|Decision-Maker Profile]] - living model of how Julian decides: strengths, the commitment gap, drivers, biases, design principles
