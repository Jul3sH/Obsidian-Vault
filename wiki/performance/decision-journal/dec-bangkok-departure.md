# Decision: When to leave Bangkok for London (16 vs 18 Sep 2026)

This is the journal record of the Bangkok departure decision: fly to London on Wednesday 16 or Friday 18 September 2026. It was written on 4 Sep 2026 from Julian's BRAIND run and his account of the post-commit reversal, and it matters beyond its size: it is the first recorded case of a committed choice being reversed without the reopen test. Read it when mining bias patterns across decisions and at the Review-on retrospective.

- **Decide-by:** not recorded (the flight booking acted as the de-facto forcing function)
- **Review-on:** 2026-09-21 (first weekend back: did Sophia join the club, was there a game on Sat 19 Sep, and was the earlier return worth what it gave up?)
- **Framework:** BRAIND (the default framework - see [[choosing-a-decision-framework]] and [[brain-brand-framework]]), previously written as "BRAINED" in [[dec-uk-move]]; BRAIND is the standard spelling from 4 Sep 2026.
- **The decision:** Leave Bangkok for London on Friday 18 Sep or Wednesday 16 Sep, both on air miles. The ideal day, Thursday 17 (back for Friday, around all weekend for Sophia), has no air-miles availability, and paying cash one-way is expensive with two suitcases. Context: the plan is to leave Hong Kong on the Wednesday afternoon (keeping the morning with Joanne) and spend two nights in Bangkok for the medical checkup. Making the Wednesday 16 Sep BKK-London flight instead means leaving Hong Kong at 8 am Tuesday (the only premium-economy air-miles HK-BKK flight with the two-bag allowance), losing more Hong Kong time and the goodbye morning with Joanne.

## B - Benefits

- Breathing space to get everything done that needs doing in Hong Kong
- More quality time with Joanne
- Time to spend individually with Scammal, Stephan, Justin, Fred
- Two nights in Bangkok
- Removes the risk of missing the medical
- No rushed final morning

## R - Risks and cons

- Cathay HKG-BKK costs an extra 10,000 miles for business class to carry two bags, as premium economy isn't available on the Wednesday
- BA costs double miles to travel Friday, as Wednesday is off-peak
- Sophia will miss me more and I will be able to do less at the weekend
- Roughly an extra HK$2,500 for the extra night in Bangkok
- As Joanne will stay with me, staying longer could complicate saying goodbye without her asking for a relationship commitment
- Clodagh will start causing trouble and saying she will stay longer if she knows I am away the entire week
- More guilt in general
- More time to dwell on leaving

## A - Alternatives

- Don't go: miss the medical and Bangkok
- Travel Wednesday: less guilt
- Travel Friday: more cash, less miles
- Money mitigation: don't spend loads when there if feeling guilty
- Clodagh mitigation: be firm, she can't control me

## I - Intuition

This is all about guilt, and largely about not getting back for Sophia. The feeling: I should be coming back to the UK ASAP, changing gear and looking for work, not having a holiday, not spending more money and air miles, not leaving Sophia longer without me when she will miss me so much. The gut says Bangkok is being done for the sake of it because I'm leaving Asia; the medical tests actually needed (cancer markers etc.) could probably be done online in the UK for a similar price. The gut also says the last few solo Bangkok trips felt a bit pointless.

Mitigations of the gut concerns: air miles (transfer more Amex points to BA and Cathay); Sophia (will be busy and survive); money (suck it up, last blowout); Stephan (can't make decisions, will never advocate for me); Fred (quick visit); Justin (meet him in DB); Scammal (will find time); Bangkok (will find ways to entertain myself, even if it's just relaxing).

## N - Need more time (showstopper checks)

- Check BKK hospital availability for Wednesday for the medical checkup
- Schedule all activities in the diary to see if Wednesday is feasible
- Book the Friday flight (tickets are limited) and do final checks

## D - Decision

**Committed choice: "Go Friday and live with feelings of guilt."** (commit date not recorded - itself a lesson; a few days before 3 Sep). Explicitly against intuition, with the analysis-wins rule applied and the guilt named as the cost to carry.

## Reversal log

- **2026-09-03 (evening) - REVERSED to Wednesday 16 Sep.** Trigger: a message from a football club saying games are on Saturdays, prompting the realisation that missing that Saturday could mean Sophia misses three consecutive Saturdays of games. Neither her joining the club nor a game on that Saturday was confirmed. The Friday flight was cancelled essentially as soon as the trigger came in: no F-N-M-T test, no 48h stand-down. **Julian's own read (4 Sep): the message was only the trigger.** The underlying driver was the whole Intuition section reasserting itself - should be getting back, should be worrying about finding a job, shouldn't be spending money, Bangkok is being done for the sake of it and probably won't even be enjoyed - not the Sophia line alone. The reversal was the original intuition winning via the first available trigger, not a new analysis.
- **Retro F-N-M-T (run 4 Sep, after the fact):** **F** - the message is a fact, but the harm ("she misses three games") is a forecast resting on two unconfirmed contingencies: weak. **N** - genuinely new information class (the club schedule was not knowable at commit): passes. **M** - only material if a game is confirmed: fails as of the evening of the reversal. **T** - "Sophia will miss me more and I will be able to do less at the weekend" was already on the risk list above and named in Intuition as the driving fear: fails. **Verdict: the reversal would not have passed the gate on the day it was made.** It was a pre-named fear carried over the line by one genuinely new but unconfirmed fact.

- **Committed choice (current, dated):** **Fly Wednesday 16 September 2026 - committed 2026-09-03 by cancelling the Friday booking** (a real behavioural act, so the reversal is itself anchored).

## Retrospective

*Process retrospective, as of 4 Sep 2026 (outcome retrospective due on Review-on, 21 Sep):*

**The key insight: the contention point is committing AGAINST intuition.** The commit overrode the gut but never dismantled it. The mitigation list patched each gut concern logistically (transfer more points, Sophia will survive, suck up the spend) but never tested the intuition's checkable claims as claims: "the last few solo Bangkok trips felt pointless" is lived evidence, not a mood; "the medical could be done online in the UK for similar money" is a checkable fact. An intuition carrying testable claims deserved [[belief-assumption-testing]] before being overridden, not a coping plan after. An against-intuition commitment where the gut never signs the verdict is inherently unstable: it sits waiting for a trigger, and any plausible one will do. The football message was simply the first to arrive. As of 4 Sep, post-reversal, Julian still endorses the intuition (doesn't need more Bangkok time, probably wouldn't enjoy it, doing it for the sake of it) - consistent with the reversal being the intuition winning, not the trigger mattering.

- **What worked:** a full BRAIND run before committing; the gut named separately and the override made knowingly, in writing; showstopper checks defined; an honest self-diagnosis within a day of the reversal ("I may have just made a rash decision because I was feeling so guilty, and this was the first real test").
- **What failed:** the decision lived outside the journal, so nothing defended it: [[dec-uk-move]] survived four wobbles because the lock protocol had jurisdiction, and this one reversed on the first trigger. The reversal skipped the F-N-M-T test and the 48h stand-down, matching the [[decision-maker-profile|profile]]'s un-deciding-within-~72h pattern exactly.
- **The missing piece at commit time:** a pre-registered reopen condition. "IF a confirmed Saturday game lands before 16 Sep, THEN switch to Wednesday" was available on the day of the commit and would have made the football message a clean, pre-authorised branch instead of a wobble that won. The UK move did this with the TTI gate; this decision did not.
- **The bias:** guilt is the family-decision twin of loss-aversion in money decisions, and here it fused with the get-back-and-earn anxiety into one intuition cluster. "Live with the guilt" named the cost but was not a mitigation; it left the commitment undefended against exactly the trigger that arrived.
- **Candidate rule for the [[commitment-lock-protocol|lock protocol]]:** when committing against intuition, either (a) convert the intuition into testable claims and refute them in writing before locking, or (b) if they survive testing, treat the intuition as evidence and change the decision. Either way, pre-register the reopen condition, because with an unsigned gut the wobble is guaranteed.
- **Fairness note:** Wednesday is defensible on this entry's own analysis (saves double miles, ~HK$2,500, simplifies the Joanne goodbye, shortens Clodagh's window), and the intuition may simply have been right. The defect is the route, not the destination: commit, trigger, same-evening reversal is indistinguishable from a wobble winning, and next time the stakes may be higher.
