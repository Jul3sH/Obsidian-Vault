# HK Return: WhatsApp Evidence on Whether the UK Move Was Temporary

## Purpose

The messages between Julian and Clodagh that show whether the UK move with Sophia was temporary and conditional, or permanent, and whether Clodagh agreed to it, each attributed to its sender. It exists because, if the TTI job comes through, Clodagh may contest a move back to Hong Kong, and the specialist lawyer needs both sides of the record (item 3 of "Evidence to gather" in [[HK-Return-legalbrief-Fable]]).

## Main finding

- **Before 30 June 2026 the summer is written about as a trip, not a move.** No message before 30 Jun refers to Sophia moving to the UK. In May and June the plan is a summer trip: London, then Clodagh to Ireland for "the month".
- **From 30 June to 19 August the record is mixed.** Julian repeatedly said the decision was open and depended on the TTI job, and both parents treated a return to Hong Kong as possible. But in July Julian also called leaving the likely default with TTI as the exception, and Clodagh wrote as if leaving was settled.
- **From 20 August, when the job fell through, Julian's own language turns permanent** ("emigrating", "pack up my life").
- **Consent:** Clodagh signed the 8 Sep letter saying both parents consented to Sophia's removal from Hong Kong, but she disputed in July that the UK arrangement was agreed, and from 23 Aug said it needed to be agreed legally.

## The return tickets

Julian's account (8 Oct 2026): the return journey was booked separately on Asia Miles tickets, because they would be easier to cancel if the job fell through, and they were cancelled when it did. His 9 Aug message ("I had return flights lined up and only changed to 1 way + 1 way after you suggested it") is consistent with two separate bookings. The 26 Jul "1 way tickets" message and the 28 Jul 14,276 total refer to the Dublin flights for Clodagh and Sophia, not the UK move. The 20 Jul "fully flexible in both directions" Cathay figures were price quotes, not a booking, so they are not relied on here.

## Documents to obtain

- Asia Miles booking confirmation: booking date (before 30 Jul) and the 24 Aug return.
- Asia Miles cancellation record with its date. A date on or after 20 Aug fits the account; an earlier one would undercut it. Check before it goes to the lawyer.
- Both full WhatsApp exports with metadata, not this extract.

## Limits of this evidence

- Calls, images and voice notes are not in the exports; many key conversations were calls.
- Habitual residence turns on stability, not permanence; a planned temporary stay of months can still shift it (AR v RN [2015] UKSC 35, per [[HK-Return-legalbrief-Fable]]).
- The criminal offence (s1 Child Abduction Act 1984) turns on Clodagh's consent at the UK border, however temporary the stay was. This evidence does not remove the need for her consent or a court order.
- Clodagh's custody offers (2025 and January 2026) were made in arguments; a court is unlikely to treat them as consent.

## Key Takeaways

- Before 30 Jun the written record describes a summer trip; no message mentions Sophia moving to the UK.
- From 30 Jun to 19 Aug the record leans conditional but is mixed: Julian's 13 Jul "likely we will be leaving" and Clodagh's 7 Jul "if we are leaving" point the other way.
- From 20 Aug Julian's own words are permanent; the lawyer must see both.
- The separate, cancellable Asia Miles return is the strongest single piece of conditional-intent evidence; get the booking and cancellation records.
- Weak points: Julian's 13 Jul "I'll tell you what they are", 20 Jul "kidnapping" and 21 Jul "breaking that agreement" messages, Clodagh's "That was your decision, not mine", and her one-way account.
