# HK Return: WhatsApp Evidence on Whether the UK Move Was Temporary

## Purpose

The messages between Julian and Clodagh from 11 June to 7 October 2026 that show whether the UK move with Sophia was temporary and conditional, or permanent, each attributed to its sender. It exists because, if the TTI job comes through, Clodagh may contest a move back to Hong Kong, and the specialist lawyer needs both sides of the record (item 3 of "Evidence to gather" in [[HK-Return-legalbrief-Fable]]).

Sections: main finding; evidence the move was temporary or conditional; evidence it was permanent; messages Clodagh's side would use; the return tickets; documents to obtain; limits of this evidence.

> As of 8 Oct 2026: complete for the export to 7 Oct 2026; quotes verified against the export. Calls, images and voice notes are not in the export, so anything said on calls is missing. Not lawyer-reviewed.

## Main finding

The record is mixed before 20 August, not purely conditional. Julian repeatedly said the decision was open and depended on the TTI job, and both parents treated a return as possible. But in July Julian also described leaving as the likely default with TTI as the exception, and Clodagh wrote as if leaving was settled. From 20 August, when the job fell through, Julian's own language turns permanent ("emigrating", "pack up my life"). A lawyer will see all of it.

## The return tickets

Julian's account (8 Oct 2026): the return journey was booked separately on Asia Miles tickets, because they would be easier to cancel if the job fell through, and they were cancelled when it did. His 9 Aug message ("I had return flights lined up and only changed to 1 way + 1 way after you suggested it") is consistent with two separate bookings. The 26 Jul "1 way tickets" message and the 28 Jul 14,276 total refer to the Dublin flights for Clodagh and Sophia, not the UK move. The 20 Jul "fully flexible in both directions" Cathay figures were price quotes, not a booking, so they are not relied on here.

## Documents to obtain

- Asia Miles booking confirmation: booking date (before 30 Jul) and the 24 Aug return.
- Asia Miles cancellation record with its date. A date on or after 20 Aug fits the account; an earlier one would undercut it. Check before it goes to the lawyer.
- The full WhatsApp export with metadata, not this extract.

## Limits of this evidence

- Habitual residence turns on stability, not permanence; a planned temporary stay of months can still shift it (AR v RN [2015] UKSC 35, per [[HK-Return-legalbrief-Fable]]).
- The criminal offence (s1 Child Abduction Act 1984) turns on Clodagh's consent at the UK border, however temporary the stay was. This evidence does not remove the need for her consent or a court order.

## Key Takeaways

- Before 20 Aug the record leans conditional but is mixed: Julian's 13 Jul "likely we will be leaving" and Clodagh's 7 Jul "if we are leaving" point the other way.
- From 20 Aug Julian's own words are permanent; the lawyer must see both.
- The separate, cancellable Asia Miles return is the strongest single piece of conditional-intent evidence; get the booking and cancellation records.
- Weak points: Julian's own 20 Jul "kidnapping" and 21 Jul "breaking that agreement" messages, Clodagh's "That was your decision, not mine", and her one-way account.
