# HK-Return-Counterfactuals

## Purpose

This is the record of the [[counterfactual-questioning]] runs for the question in [[HK-Return-BRAIND]]: if TTI puts a written offer on the table, does Julian move back to Hong Kong or hold the committed UK move? It exists to show which factors are driving his gut feeling against going.

**Map:** Status · Key Takeaways · The feeling · Runs completed · Other questions asked · Runs still to do · Ranking (superseded) · Ranking adopted · Links · Document Log

## Status

> ⚠ **Status (29 Sep 2026):** all five runs are completed and the list is closed. With the job risk removed, Julian chooses Hong Kong. He adopted the ranking under "Ranking adopted" below on 29 Sep. It is a lean, not a decision. The runs were answered hours after a conversation with a friend that, in Julian's words, removed the guilt, so runs 3 and 5 are to be asked again on or after 1 Oct 2026 and no decision state changes before then. Julian's full answers are in the Intuition Log of [[HK-Return-BRAIND]], 29 Sep blocks.

## Key Takeaways

- Five answers to hypotheticals from one sitting are a strong lean, not proof. They were given hours after a friend told Julian to live his life, which he says removed the guilt.
- With the job risk removed, Julian chooses Hong Kong, even when the same TTI role is available from Malvern. Hong Kong is what he wants; the job risk is the obstacle.
- The pull to Hong Kong does not depend on romance. His reasons: more money, his own place, better romance options.
- Whether he could keep the job on merit once Horst steps back cannot be known now, so any plan has to work either way.
- The ranking weighs risk and money only. The non-financial benefits of Hong Kong are not in it.
- Julian's own next step is to ask Stephan how safe the role is. The remaining runs do not block that.

## The feeling

Julian, 29 Sep 2026: "My gut instinct is telling me not to do it." He named three possible causes: guilt about Mum, Clodagh and Sophia; fear of making a mistake; or a gut that knows the success rests on wishful thinking. On 28 Sep he had said: "I am very torn. The primary driver is finances." Both stand.

## Ranking (29 Sep 2026, built before runs 3 to 5, superseded as a lean)

Built by the model from Julian's answers. Julian: "I think the ranking looks right if I can't get a really good guarantee from Stephan. The only thing left is to ask Stephan outright 'Honestly speaking Stephan, how safe would I be for six years'." He then asked to revisit the ranking.

| Rank | Option | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | TTI role done from Malvern | Run 2, the good weekday picture, the advice to the friend |
| 2 | Hong Kong on a guaranteed two years, school fees included, planned return | Clears the money test and bounds the worst case |
| 3 | UK market role, likely hybrid | The bad weekday picture: "possible and workable, but probably not ideal" |

The order holds only if no strong guarantee is available. With one, the order is open. Hong Kong on the offer as written, which is the BRAIND's actual question, is not ranked because no written offer exists.

**Known weaknesses of the ranking (model, 29 Sep 2026):**

| Weakness | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| It is built from gut answers, and the sheet disagrees | The savings sheet puts Hong Kong ahead of rank 1 by about HK$1.0M at six years, or about HK$2.0M with schooling in the package. Under the rule in [[decision-maker-profile]], the analysis stands unless the gut justifies the override in writing |
| Rank 1 fits the third-option pattern | The BRAIND warns that an option avoiding the choice between Hong Kong and Malvern can look attractive for that reason alone. It has not been tested on its own merits |
| Risk is weighted above money | Julian named finances as his primary driver on 28 Sep |
| The non-financial benefits of Hong Kong are not weighed | From the BRAIND's opportunities: the Enterprise Architecture Director title, building a network, DBIS for Sophia, a more interesting way to spend his 50s, finding a partner, custody jurisdiction, testing the relationship with Joanne |
| Rank 1 may not exist | Nobody at TTI has been asked about a UK base |
| Rank 1 shares the bad weekend | Malvern with little social life applies to it in full |
| The two-year figures rest on an unverified rule | That a return after two years keeps Sophia's home fee status |
