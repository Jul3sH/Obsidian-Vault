# MM: Never Bitch

This is the Never Bitch mental model: complaining, moaning, and gossiping have no
upside and real downside, in every disguise they wear. Read it whenever you're
tempted to vent, joke at someone's expense, or offload frustration to a
colleague. Full context and evidence are in [[never-bitch-whinge-or-gossip]].

**One-liner:** Every complaint reaches its subject eventually, and jokes count.

**Reach for it when:** you're tempted to vent, moan, joke at someone's expense,
or offload to feel better.

## Key Takeaways

- Bitching has no upside and real downside, every time, no exceptions.
- It hides in disguised forms: jokes, quitting threats, shared complaints to
  build rapport, offloading to feel better.
- The fix is a pattern interrupt: catch it, use humour, then flip to a positive
  or solution frame.

## Principles

- **Venting feels like connection or relief; it's neither.** It's ammunition
  handed to someone else.
- **"Taking the piss" and quitting threats are bitching wearing a disguise.**
- **Shared-complaint rapport is fragile.** The other person has their own agenda
  and may use it against you, or simply repeat it.
- **Offloading just to feel better, when nothing actually needs fixing, only
  makes things worse.**

## Guidelines

- Catch yourself mid-complaint and flip it: whinge, humour, positive spin.
- Replace moan-language with progress-language, even with people you trust
  ("I'm making progress" not "I can't cope").
- Don't bond with someone over a shared complaint about a third party, find
  another way to build rapport.
- If you have nothing constructive to add, say nothing.

## Limitations

Doesn't mean suppress real problems. Raising an issue with someone who can act
on it is not bitching, see [[mm-bring-solutions]]. The line is whether you want
their opinion or action, not whether you're venting.
