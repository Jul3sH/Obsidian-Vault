# MM: Write It Down

This is the Write It Down mental model: whether a decision, agreement, or
escalation exists as far as anyone else is concerned if it was never put in
writing. It exists because in a political environment people rewrite
history when things go wrong, and an unwritten record cannot defend you even
when you were right. Read it before a scope change, an escalation, a
handover, or any meeting where objectives or actions are agreed.

**One-liner:** In political environments, if it isn't written down it didn't
happen.

**Reach for it when:** something contentious, a scope change, an
escalation, or a handover is happening and no written record yet exists.

## Key Takeaways

- Without an audit trail you cannot defend your position, even when you
  were right; people rewrite history when things go wrong.
- Objectives and key results need sign-off, not just discussion; scope
  changes need written confirmation with a date.
- Meeting notes decay fast; write them the same day or the detail becomes
  unreconstructable.
- A tracker or evaluation report isn't admin, it's what stops someone
  else's ass-covering from becoming your problem.
- Sending written updates even when the other party doesn't want them
  protects an accuracy that verbal-only reporting loses.

## Principles

- If it isn't written down and dated, it didn't happen, and can be
  rewritten by whoever tells the story first.
- Get visible written agreement on anything contentious so it can't be
  silently distorted later.
- Document handovers with sign-off; a poor handover is a debt that comes due
  later.
- Escalations and constraints raised verbally aren't raised until they're on
  record: if you flagged it, prove you flagged it.

## Guidelines

- Minute every meeting where objectives, actions, or scope are discussed;
  note who agreed to what, by when.
- Keep a running log of events on any contentious deliverable, reviewed
  before meetings where it might be challenged.
- Write meeting notes the same day; delay makes them unreadable and costs
  more time to reconstruct than it saved.
- When someone else's updates upward are inaccurate, send your own written
  version even if they don't want you to.

## Limitations

Documentation protects you when facts are disputed; it doesn't substitute
for the harder conversation, escalation, or pushback the underlying issue
may actually need.
