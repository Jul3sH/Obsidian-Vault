# MM: Bring Solutions

This is the Bring Solutions mental model: a problem raised without a mitigation
is just a complaint with a business excuse. Read it before you raise an issue
with someone senior to you, or before escalating anything you haven't already
tried to fix. Full context and evidence are in
[[managing-up-bring-solutions-communicate-success]].

**One-liner:** A problem without a mitigation is a complaint.

**Reach for it when:** you're about to raise an issue upward, or tempted to
escalate before trying to fix it yourself.

## Key Takeaways

- Bosses want delivery, not deliberation about how to deliver properly.
- Never bring a problem without a proposed mitigation, or you're just handing
  over a drama.
- Only voice a problem when you want the listener's opinion or action on it, not
  to vent.

## Principles

- **Bosses expect solutions, not problems.** Deliberating out loud reads as a
  credibility risk, not diligence.
- **A problem raised with no mitigation, and no sign you've exhausted your own
  options first, gets dismissed as a sob story.**
- **Don't tell people your problems unless you want their opinion or their
  action on it.** Otherwise it's whingeing with a business excuse.

## Guidelines

- Before raising anything, ask: have I proposed a mitigation, or am I just
  describing the problem?
- Show what you've already tried before escalating.
- If you don't need the listener's help or opinion, don't raise it as a problem
  at all.

## Limitations

Doesn't apply to a genuine blocker outside your authority, that still needs
immediate, formal escalation. The model governs how a problem is framed, it's
not a reason to sit on one that needs raising now.
