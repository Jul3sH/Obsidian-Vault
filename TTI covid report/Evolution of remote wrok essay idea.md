Here’s a strong, essay-ready structure + detailed bullet points + case studies you can expand into ~1500 words. I’ve organised it like a university essay (intro → arguments → case studies → evaluation → conclusion), and tied everything clearly to COVID-19. 
 
🧠 Essay Title Idea 
 
“How has 2020 affected the evolution of remote work?” 
🧱 1. Introduction (≈150–200 words) 
Key points to include: 
Remote work has diversely amplified the traditional workplace since its enabling through digital technology. it has rapidly evolved in the past decade due to social distancing rules imposed by world governments during the 2020 covid 19 pandemic. this sudden change forced the majority of business and companies to morph their operations from traditional offices to homes and personal working environments this had a heavy impact on all organisations as they faced many cons and rapid change in such a short period of time although some businesses may have experience with remote work prior to the pandemic for many it was new for both employers and employees. 2020 did not create remote work but it dramatically escalated its use and evolution to what it is today. 
 
 
•Define remote work (working outside traditional office, enabled by digital tech). 

•Brief mention: remote work existed pre-2020 but was limited and often optional. 

•Introduce COVID-19 (2020) as the turning point: 

•Forced global lockdowns → sudden shift to working from home. 

•Thesis idea: 

•2020 did not create remote work, but dramatically accelerated and transformed its adoption, structure, and long-term role in the global economy.
